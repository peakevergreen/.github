Peak Evergreen is a small AI consulting and software practice based in Fort Myers, Florida. We work with people and teams across Southwest Florida and remotely nationwide on practical AI, automation, custom software, and websites.

## About

Kevin Mullin founded Peak Evergreen after about twenty years in software. His work has moved through visual effects, games, and production AI systems:

- **Visual effects.** Built tools and workflows for artists and worked on rigging and cloth for feature films and animation, including at Hydraulx.
- **Games.** Lead Technical Artist at Sony Computer Entertainment, writing Python tools for game teams. Also wrote pipeline documentation used to train technical artists.
- **AI in production.** Senior developer at Employ, shipping LLM-based candidate matching for recruiting platforms that serve millions of users.

Kevin leads each project personally, from the first conversation through delivery.

## What we work on

- **AI integration.** Choosing tools, setting strategy, and training teams to use AI well.
- **Automation.** Connecting forms, CRMs, and spreadsheets so data stops getting copied by hand.
- **Software.** Custom apps, internal tools, and APIs, from prototype to production.
- **Web.** Websites and landing pages.
